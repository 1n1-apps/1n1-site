features:
  - title: Today
    text: Your day on one screen. Events, tasks, habits, alarms and logs together, with a strip to move between days.
  - title: Week and calendar
    text: Week lays days side by side with the hours running down. Calendar scrolls through the months, each day showing what's on it.
  - title: Alarms
    text: Exact alarms that ring on the lock screen and through silent mode. While one rings, it shows what's next in your day.
  - title: Steps
    text: Break a task or habit into steps, two levels deep. A step can be a tick, a number, a choice, a duration or a timed session.
  - title: Logs
    text: Keep track of your weight, mood or sleep without a schedule. Record whenever you like; nothing is ever due.
  - title: Tracking
    text: Rates and streaks for every habit, repeating task, log and repeating alarm.
  - title: Weather
    text: The forecast on your week and on your home screen, from the Norwegian Meteorological Institute.
  - title: Widgets
    text: Eight free home-screen widgets, including Today, Clock, Up next, the Week strip and the Month grid.
  - title: Make it yours
    text: Over 450 icons and twelve colours for your items. Light, warm or dark, or one for the day and one for the night.
  - title: Your data stays yours
    text: Everything stays on your phone, with no account. Export it to a file, and import it on a new phone.
